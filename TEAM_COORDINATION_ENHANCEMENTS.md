### 3. **Leader Assignment Interface** 📋
Team leaders and coordinators can assign SOS reports to team members:

#### Features:
- **Select SOS Report**: Dropdown of all unassigned emergencies
- **Select Team Member**: Choose from available team
- **Set Priority**: Critical, High, Medium, Low
- **Add Notes**: Instructions for the assigned member
- **One-Click Assign**: Submit assignment instantly

#### Assignment Methods:
1. **From Overview Tab**: Click "Assign Now" on any SOS card
2. **From Assign Tab**: Use full assignment form
3. **From SOS Modal**: Click "Assign to Team Member" button

## 📱 How to Use

### As Team Member:
1. **View SOS Reports**:
   - Go to Overview tab
   - See all unassigned emergencies
   - Click any card for full details
   - View location on Google Maps

2. **Check Team Status**:
   - Go to Team Members tab
   - See who's available
   - Check who's assigned to what
   - Get contact information

3. **Manage Your Assignments**:
   - Go to My Assignments tab
   - Update incident status
   - Add progress logs
   - Track completion

### As Team Leader:
1. **Assign Emergencies**:
   - Click "Assign Now" on SOS card, OR
   - Go to Assign Incidents tab
   - Select SOS report
   - Choose team member
   - Set priority and add notes
   - Submit assignment

2. **Coordinate Team**:
   - View all active assignments
   - See team member status
   - Monitor progress
   - Reassign if needed

## 🎯 Coordination Workflow

### Emergency Response Flow:
```
1. SOS Report Received
   ↓
2. Appears in "Unassigned SOS Reports"
   ↓
3. Team Leader Reviews Details
   ↓
4. Leader Assigns to Available Member
   ↓
5. Member Receives Assignment
   ↓
6. Member Updates Status (Dispatched → En Route → On Scene)
   ↓
7. Member Adds Logs
   ↓
8. Member Marks as Resolved
```
